# Definition and Validation of Nematode Nigon Units for `chromadorea_odb12`

This directory documents the re-definition and cross-validation of nematode ancestral linkage groups (Nigon units **A**, **B**, **C**, **D**, **E**, **N**, and **X**) for the modern OrthoDB v12 / BUSCO v5 lineage `chromadorea_odb12` (1,126 orthologs).

---

## 1. Overview & Motivation

The original Nigon element dictionary in `vis_ALG` (`data/gene2Nigon_busco20200927.tsv.gz`) was calibrated for BUSCO v4 using the `nematoda_odb10` lineage (3,131 loci), where 2,298 loci were classified into Nigon units (Gonzalez de la Rosa et al., *G3*, 2021).

Modern chromosome-level nematode assemblies generated by the Tree of Life (ToL) and Darwin Tree of Life (DToL) projects standardly use the updated `chromadorea_odb12` BUSCO lineage (1,126 loci). Here, we replicated the unsupervised clustering methodology of Gonzalez de la Rosa et al. (2021) across **77 chromosome-level assemblies** to derive a de novo, publication-grade Nigon classification for `chromadorea_odb12`.

---

## 2. Methodology

1. **Assembly Mining & Inventory**:
   - Screened all 144 nematode assemblies under the Sanger Tree of Life repository.
   - Identified **77 verified chromosome-level assemblies** with INSDC chromosomal mappings and complete single-copy `chromadorea_odb12` BUSCO results (documented in `data/tol_77_chromosomal_assemblies_inventory.tsv`).

2. **Phylogenetically Balanced Panel Selection (17 Genera)**:
   - To prevent phylogenetic distortion from heavily sampled genera (e.g. 24 *Caenorhabditis* or 13 *Oscheius* species), we selected 17 non-redundant representative genera spanning the phylogenetic breadth of Chromadorea:
     - **Rhabditina**: *Caenorhabditis kamaaina*, *Oscheius myriophilus*, *Auanema rhodensis*, *Litoditis marina*
     - **Diplogasteromorpha**: *Koerneria pararmatus*
     - **Strongylida**: *Cooperia oncophora*, *Dictyocaulus viviparus*, *Heligmosomum mixtum*
     - **Spirurina**: *Acanthocheilonema viteae*, *Dirofilaria immitis*, *Thelazia callipaeda*, *Spirocerca lupi*
     - **Tylenchina**: *Acrobeloides maximus* (Cephalobidae), *Panagrellus redivivus* (HAP1), *Panagrolaimus detritophagus*, *Turbatrix aceti*, *Sphaerularia bombi*
   - Strictly enforced 1 primary accession per genus and collapsed phased diploid haplotypes to eliminate duplicate gene counting.

3. **Core Presence Filter ($\ge 80\%$)**:
   - **1,098 out of 1,126 BUSCO loci ($97.5\%$)** were present as single-copy markers in $\ge 14$ of the 17 panel taxa.

4. **Pairwise Dice Distance Matrix**:
   - For all pairs of loci $(i, j)$, Dice similarity was computed from chromosomal co-allocation:
     $$S(i, j) = \frac{2 \cdot |T_{\text{same}}(i, j)|}{|T(i)| + |T(j)|}, \quad D(i, j) = 1 - S(i, j)$$

5. **Unsupervised Clustering (PAM & CLARA)**:
   - Evaluated partitioning around medoids (PAM) and CLARA across $k = 2 \dots 10$.

---

## 3. Key Results

### Unsupervised Global Peak at $k = 7$
The average silhouette width across $k = 2 \dots 10$ revealed that **$k = 7$ strictly emerges de novo as the global maximum peak ($s = 0.675$)**, proving the 7 ancestral linkage groups of Nematoda without supervised priors:

## 4. Phylum-Wide Conservation (Monhysterida & Enoplea Outgroups)

We cross-tabulated the chromosomal allocations of basal Monhysterida (*Daptonema setosum*, $n=7$) and Enoplea (*Echinomermella matsi*, $n=7$):
- In *Daptonema setosum* (Monhysterida): Every autosome corresponds 1-to-1 to a canonical Nigon unit with **$79\% – 90\%$ purity** (`SUPER_2` = A, `SUPER_4` = B, `SUPER_1` = C, `SUPER_3` = D, `SUPER_7` = E, `SUPER_6` = N, `SUPER_5` = X).
- In *Echinomermella matsi* (Enoplea / Mermithida, diverged $>500$ Mya): `SUPER_1` is **$92.6\%$ pure Nigon A**, `SUPER_3` is **$84.8\%$ pure Nigon D**, `SUPER_6` is **$90.1\%$ pure Nigon N**, `SUPER_7` is **$82.7\%$ pure Nigon E**, and `SUPER_5` is **$83.8\%$ pure Nigon X**.
- **Conclusion**: Nigon units are not Rhabditida-specific; they represent the ancestral karyotypic units of the entire phylum Nematoda.

## 5. Direct Gene-Level Physical Coordinate Overlap Benchmark

We intersected physical genomic coordinates `(Sequence, Start, End)` between `chromadorea_odb12` and `nematoda_odb10` across reference genomes (*Caenorhabditis kamaaina*, *Oscheius myriophilus*, *Litoditis marina*, *Auanema rhodensis*, *Acrobeloides maximus*, *Koerneria pararmatus*, *Cooperia oncophora*):
- Total physical genomic interval overlaps: **5,442**
- Unique overlapping gene pairs: **971**
- Pairs with both lineages classified: **781**
- **Exact 7x7 Diagonal Concordance**: **$761 / 781 = 97.44\%$**!
  - Nigon A: $210 / 214$ ($98.1\%$)
  - Nigon B: $116 / 118$ ($98.3\%$)
  - Nigon C: $162 / 166$ ($97.6\%$)
  - Nigon D: $75 / 78$ ($96.2\%$)
  - Nigon E: $92 / 94$ ($97.9\%$)
  - Nigon N: $70 / 74$ ($94.6\%$)
  - Nigon X: $36 / 37$ ($97.3\%$)

### Biological Origin of Moderate Silhouette Loci ($s_i < 0.35$)
Loci with silhouette widths $s_i < 0.35$ were **$74.5\%$ unassigned in the 2020 definitions**, proving that these loci represent genuinely promiscuous, frequently translocated loci that fail strict consensus colocation thresholds.

## 7. Data Files in `vis_ALG/data/`

1. **`data/gene2Nigon_chromadorea_odb12.tsv.gz`** (Canonical 2-column drop-in, 1,120 assigned loci, zero `NA` rows, header `Orthogroup\tnigon`):
   Recommended for standard visualization in `vis_ALGs.R` and `app.R`.

   Ultra-conservative set matching the 2021 *G3* paper thresholding where noisy loci are omitted.
3. **`data/gene2Nigon_busco_chromadorea_odb12.tsv.gz`** (Full 5-tier master table, 1,126 loci):
   Documents all assignments with explicit confidence tiers (`core_strict_ge_050`, `core_high_conf_ge_035`, `core_moderate_lt_035`, `consensus_high`, `unassigned_fuzzy`) and exact silhouette widths.
